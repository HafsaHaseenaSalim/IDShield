How an attempt is scored

1

Verification API

Document, liveness and credential checks.

2

Rule layer

Velocity, reputation, attribute consistency, cross-record links, document forensics.

3

Model layer

A classifier over those same signals, validated on held-out data.

4

Decision

One score, a full reason chain, and one of three outcomes.

ALLOW0–29

Proceed normally.

STEP-UP30–69

Challenge before proceeding. Friction, not denial.

BLOCK70–100

Stop the attempt and record the evidence.
